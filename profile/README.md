### Why Tkvaa

At many small businesses, appointments still arrive as phone calls and WhatsApp messages. The owner answers between clients, keeps the diary on paper or from memory, and loses the hour when someone forgets to come. Tkvaa turns that into a link: clients book themselves at any hour, and every time slot can be taken only once.

### What it does

**For clients**

- Book from the business's link at any hour with a name and a phone number. No app to download, no account to create.
- See only the times that are really free, for the service and the staff member they choose.
- Cancel or reschedule from the same link, and add the appointment to their phone's calendar.

**For businesses**

- A booking page with your services, prices, hours, photos and reviews, shared as a link or a QR code.
- One calendar for online, phone and walk-in bookings, in day, week and list views.
- Automatic WhatsApp confirmations, and a reminder before each appointment.
- A waitlist that offers a cancelled slot to the next client.
- A client list with visit history, private notes and no-show tracking.
- Staff, chairs and rooms with their own calendars, and group classes with a seat limit.
- An iPhone app with push notifications and the next appointment on the lock screen.

### Who it's for

Any business that works by appointment: barbershops and hair salons, nail, lash and brow studios, skin care and laser clinics, massage, medical and dental clinics, fitness studios and classes, pet grooming, optics, car washes and more. Choose your business type when you sign up, and Tkvaa adapts its services and wording to it.

### Built for Israel

- **Hebrew and Arabic, right to left.** Both languages are part of the product, not a translation layer.
- **Free for clients.** Businesses choose a monthly plan, with no commission per booking.
- **Web and iPhone.** Works in any phone browser, and on the App Store in Israel.
